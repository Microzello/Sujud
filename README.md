# Sujud

Prayer times for where you are, and nothing else. For iPhone, iPad, Apple Watch and Mac.

- Fajr, Sunrise, Dhuhr, Asr, Maghrib and Isha, with a countdown to the next prayer
- Works offline: every time is calculated on the device, and nothing is collected or stored
- Notifications, widgets, Live Activities and a Qibla compass
- The calculation method is in Settings › Apps › Sujud (Muslim World League by default)
